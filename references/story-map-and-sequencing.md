# Story Map and Sequencing

Create this map before moving or rewriting pages in final-polish work:

| Slide | Chapter | Commercial question | Conclusion | Why here | Enables next | Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Sequencing checks

- A page must not state the final “next step” before the deck establishes why the strategy and mechanism are credible.
- Do not repeat strategy, cadence, mechanism, or result as if each were a new chapter. Select one canonical page for each job; supporting pages add proof, not restatement.
- Each divider must state the Part, question solved, and audience takeaway.
- Enter platforms, media, or execution ownership only after the relevant strategic judgment is established.
- A chapter transition must answer: what have we now proved, and why does that make the next decision necessary?

## Commercial title test

A title is ready when a client can understand the page decision without listening to the presenter. A subtitle can state evidence or boundary; body content explains the mechanism or action.
