# Visual Finish Specification

Apply this after identifying the existing deck’s visual system. It is a finishing specification, not an invitation to replace a client-approved design.

## System additions

- Define separate tokens for light and dark pages: title, body, secondary copy, numbers, rules, fills, and emphasis.
- Define standard geometry for recurring tables, three-column pages, image/text modules, headers, and bottom bars.
- Define image role by claim: product, audience lifestyle, research/data, mechanism, or conceptual illustration.

## Full-size review checklist

- Repeated modules share grid, header height, image height, gutter, line-height, and footer/bottom-bar alignment.
- Do not run the same layout silhouette for more than two consecutive pages; the third needs a purposeful rhythm change.
- Dark pages do not inherit low-contrast greys from light pages; title, labels, rules, and explanatory copy stay clearly tiered.
- Crop to the visual subject. Do not cut faces awkwardly, crowd a subject into an edge, or leave rough cut-out edges.
- Inspect full-size renders for overflow, irregular wraps, optical alignment, clipping, chart contrast, and usable whitespace.

## Forbidden shortcuts

- A product packshot cannot stand in for an audience, trend, mechanism, or research claim.
- Decorative cards cannot replace information design.
- A thumbnail/contact-sheet check cannot pass visual QA on its own.
